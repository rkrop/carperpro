## PROMPT (copy from here)

Design a **mobile application** (mobile-first, Android priority) for an **auto parts store** in Mexico. The app is a fast-search catalog with integrated checkout. Its promise is: **Fast, Simple, and Efficient.**

The app's UI text and content should be in **Mexican Spanish** (the end users are in Mexico), even though these instructions are in English.

### Business context
- Catalog of 8,000+ products (auto parts) and 80,000+ units in stock.
- Users must find the correct part in seconds and be confident it's compatible with their vehicle.
- Checkout in just a few taps, using payment methods common in Mexico.

### Users
- **Mechanics / repair shops:** search fast, sometimes already know the part number. Frequent buyers.
- **Car owners:** don't know the part number; they search by their vehicle's make, model, and year.

### Visual style
- A **professional, trustworthy, automotive-industrial** look and feel.
- Palette: **dark graphite/black tones + a strong accent color** (red, or industrial blue/orange). High contrast so it's readable in sunlight or inside a workshop.
- Clear, generously sized typography (users may have dirty hands or be in a hurry).
- Large, tappable buttons with obvious visual hierarchy. Simple iconography.
- A sense of **speed and efficiency**, without visual clutter.

### Screens to design

1. **Home**
   - Prominent search bar at the top ("Busca tu refacción…" / "Search for your part…").
   - Quick access to "Search by my vehicle."
   - A "My saved vehicle" card or selector.
   - Popular categories as cards (brakes, filters, suspension, engine, electrical, etc.).
   - Featured or on-sale products.

2. **Search by vehicle**
   - Step-by-step flow: Make → Model → Year → Engine/trim.
   - Fast-selection design (lists or chips), minimal taps.
   - Result: a filtered catalog showing ONLY compatible parts, each with a visible "Compatible with your [vehicle]" badge.

3. **Search results**
   - Product list as cards: photo, name, brand, price, availability ("In stock"), and an "Add" button.
   - Filters (side or top): part brand, price, availability.
   - Typo-tolerant search.

4. **Product detail**
   - Large product photo.
   - Name, part number, brand, and equivalents.
   - Highlighted price and stock level.
   - Clear compatibility badge and an expandable list of compatible vehicles.
   - A sticky, always-visible "Add to cart" button.

5. **Cart**
   - Product list with editable quantities.
   - Clear subtotal, taxes, and total.
   - Large "Continue to payment" button.

6. **Checkout / Payment (as few steps as possible, ideally 2–3 screens)**
   - Delivery selection: "Pick up at store" or "Home delivery."
   - Payment methods: card, OXXO/cash, bank transfer (SPEI), pay on pickup.
   - Final summary and a "Confirm purchase" button.
   - Order-success confirmation screen.

7. **My account**
   - "My vehicles" saved (so users don't re-enter them).
   - Order history with a one-tap "Reorder" button.

### UX principles (mandatory)
- Anyone, with or without mechanical knowledge, should be able to buy without help.
- The fewest possible taps from search to purchase.
- The search bar and the buy button should always be accessible.
- Clear states: loading, no results, out of stock, error.

Deliver the design for these screens with a consistent component system (buttons, cards, inputs) and a navigable flow between them.
